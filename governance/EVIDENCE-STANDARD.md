# Evidence standard

- SOURCE: direct observation of paper, repository, or official documentation.
- REPORTED: authors' results, not independently reproduced.
- REPRODUCED: independently executed with recorded settings, raw outputs, and commands.
- PROPOSED: hypothesis or design, not established result.
- OPEN: unresolved or unavailable evidence.

Every substantive finding needs a stable ID, evidence classification, primary URL, version/date or commit and file/line references where available, limitations, applicability, review status, and related tasks. Preserve benchmark definitions, sample sizes and denominators. Do not compare incompatible metrics directly. Distinguish synthetic checks from real footage and human review. No fabricated measurements, citations or licensing assumptions. Check dataset and checkpoint licenses separately. Record negative results and superseded claims.
