|---|---|---|
| VidChapters-7M | 817K videos, 7M user chapters, mixed online videos | https://antoyang.github.io/vidchapters.html links annotations, ASR, split IDs and processed data. Raw media not guaranteed; creator leakage and coarse labels. Exact duration distribution/storage/license not verified. |

| GraphTP | 122 movies, 84 train/38 test, ~17k scenes, ~1500 TP events | pinned README; features offered, raw copyrighted movies restricted. Not livestream boundaries. |
