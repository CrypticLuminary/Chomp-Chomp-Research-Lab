# Source inventory
33 distinct works/dataset documentation screened through primary publication pages or official project descriptions; detailed reading is narrower. No systematic database review, no inference. Bibliographic entries are not claims of full-paper reading.
1. [Chapter-Llama](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Ventura_Chapter-Llama_Efficient_Chaptering_in_Hour-Long_Videos_with_LLMs_CVPR_2025_paper.html) — Ventura et al., CVPR 2025. Timestamped ASR and selected-frame captions; flat chapter prediction.
2. [VidChapters-7M](https://antoyang.github.io/vidchapters.html) — Yang et al., NeurIPS 2023 D&B. User chapters; coarse training supervision.
3. [ChunkyEdit](https://research.adobe.com/publication/chunkyedit-text-first-video-interview-editing-via-chunking/) — CHI 2024. Human-assisted thematic grouping, not automated final edits.
4. [Automatic Story Segmentation for TV News Video Using Multiple Modalities](https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/732514) — Dumont and Quénot, 2012. Broadcast-specific cues and modality combinations.
5. [Leveraging Summarization for Unsupervised Dialogue Topic Segmentation](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.291/) — Artemiev et al., Findings NAACL 2024. Summarization for noisy spoken dialogue; compare raw vs compressed.
6. [GEBD](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Shou_Generic_Event_Boundary_Detection_A_Benchmark_for_Event_Segmentation_ICCV_2021_paper.html) — Shou et al., ICCV 2021. Taxonomy-free visual boundary annotations.
7. [STEPs](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Shah_STEPs_Self-Supervised_Key_Step_Extraction_and_Localization_from_Unlabeled_Procedural_ICCV_2023_paper.html) — Shah et al., ICCV 2023. Self-supervised procedural visual feature learning and clustering.
8. [GraphTP](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/17607) — Papalampidi et al., AAAI 2021. Sparse scene graph for turning-point identification; assumes scenes.
9. [NEST](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.2512/) — Asgarov et al., EMNLP 2026. Nested grounded narrative events and relationships.
10. [QMSum](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.472/) — Zhong et al., NAACL 2021. Meeting query-summary spans; useful for context retrieval, not direct segmentation model.
11. [AMI Meeting Corpus](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/annotation.shtml) — AMI consortium. Dialogue acts, topics, summaries and links.
12. [TextTiling](https://aclanthology.org/J97-1003/) — Hearst, CL 1997. Lexical cohesion gaps; unsupervised baseline.
13. [Bayesian Unsupervised Topic Segmentation](https://aclanthology.org/D08-1035/) — Eisenstein and Barzilay, EMNLP 2008. Probabilistic partition objective.
14. [CausalChapter](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.635/) — Duan et al., EMNLP 2026. Local dependency shift and cross-segment support via interventions.
15. [ARC-Chapter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14349) — Pu et al., preprint 2025. Structured chapters, multiple summary granularities, modality dropout.
16. [Multimodal Fusion and Coherence Modeling for Video Topic Segmentation](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.904/) — Yu et al., Findings ACL 2025. Learned lecture topic segmentation.
17. [Multimodal segmentation and labeling of lecture recordings](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00521-025-11740-2) — Neural Computing and Applications, 2026. VAD variable-length units then hierarchical classification.
18. [TIAGE](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-emnlp.145/) — Xie et al., Findings EMNLP 2021. Human topic-shift annotations.
19. [Advances in domain independent linear text segmentation](https://aclanthology.org/A00-2004/) — Choi, NAACL 2000. Similarity rank and partition baseline.
20. [Text Segmentation as a Supervised Learning Task](https://aclanthology.org/N18-2075/) — Koshorek et al., NAACL 2018. Learned document boundary classification.
21. [UBoCo](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Kang_UBoCo_Unsupervised_Boundary_Contrastive_Learning_for_Generic_Event_Boundary_Detection_CVPR_2022_paper.html) — Kang et al., CVPR 2022. Temporal self-similarity with contrastive boundary learning.
22. [DiffGEBD](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Hwang_Generic_Event_Boundary_Detection_via_Denoising_Diffusion_ICCV_2025_paper.html) — Hwang et al., ICCV 2025. Diffusion over plausible boundaries; uncertainty direction.
23. [Motion Aware Self-Supervision for GEBD](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/html/K._Motion_Aware_Self-Supervision_for_Generic_Event_Boundary_Detection_WACV_2023_paper.html) — WACV 2023. Motion cues beyond appearance.
24. [What's in the Flow?](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2024/html/Gothe_Whats_in_the_Flow_Exploiting_Temporal_Motion_Cues_for_Unsupervised_WACV_2024_paper.html) — Gothe et al., WACV 2024. Unsupervised motion-based event boundaries.
25. [Vid2Seq](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Yang_Vid2Seq_Large-Scale_Pretraining_of_a_Visual_Language_Model_for_Dense_CVPR_2023_paper.html) — Yang et al., CVPR 2023. ASR sentence boundaries as pseudo supervision; timestamp-caption decoding.
26. [PDVC](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Wang_End-to-End_Dense_Video_Captioning_With_Parallel_Decoding_ICCV_2021_paper.html) — Wang et al., ICCV 2021. Set prediction of intervals and captions.
27. [DIBS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Wu_DIBS_Enhancing_Dense_Video_Captioning_with_Unlabeled_Videos_via_Pseudo_CVPR_2024_paper.html) — Wu et al., CVPR 2024. Refines noisy pseudo events; potential label bootstrap.
28. [MS-TCN](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Abu_Farha_MS-TCN_Multi-Stage_Temporal_Convolutional_Network_for_Action_Segmentation_CVPR_2019_paper.html) — Abu Farha and Gall, CVPR 2019. Supervised temporal action labels and smoothing.
29. [Improving Unsupervised Dialogue Topic Segmentation with Utterance-Pair Coherence Scoring](https://aclanthology.org/2021.sigdial-1.18/) — Xing and Carenini, SIGDIAL 2021. Utterance-pair coherence, beyond lexical overlap.
30. [A few shades of supervision for discourse segmentation](https://aclanthology.org/2025.dnd-16.5/) — 2025. EDUs in spontaneous French speech, disfluency and prosody.
31. [PELT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.1438) — Killick, Fearnhead and Eckley, JASA 2012. Pruned exact change-point DP, conditional expected linear cost.
32. [A Large-Scale Corpus for Conversation Disentanglement](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1374/) — Kummerfeld et al., ACL 2019. Interleaved thread relations, not flat partition.
33. [A Critique and Improvement of an Evaluation Metric for Text Segmentation](https://aclanthology.org/J02-1002/) — Pevzner and Hearst, CL 2002. WindowDiff, Pk limitations.
