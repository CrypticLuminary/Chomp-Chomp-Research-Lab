# VidChapters-7M
Yang et al., NeurIPS 2023 D&B; primary source: https://antoyang.github.io/vidchapters.html

817K videos / about 7M user-created chapters; scraped labels rather than independent annotation. Tasks: generate boundaries+titles, titles given boundaries, grounding given title. Official project links processed data/ASR, split IDs, checkpoints. Content availability and per-video rights vary; duration/split exact counts and license terms require verification. Coarse creator navigation labels not fine-thought gold. ADAPT metadata structure; REPRODUCE only permitted subset.

Evidence: SOURCE for directly described mechanisms; REPORTED for author numbers; PROPOSED for transfer decision. No independent model reproduction. Unspecified parameters, loss, metrics, exact hardware and rights remain OPEN rather than assumed.
