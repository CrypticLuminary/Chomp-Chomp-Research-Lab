# QMSum
Zhong et al., NAACL 2021; primary source: https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.472/

QMSum 1,808 query-summary pairs across 232 meetings; AMI has 100h total recording corpus with dialogue acts, topic segments and summary links (coverage varies). Query-conditioned spans are not independent event boundaries. ADAPT annotation layers, use only properly licensed portions; no creator-level live domain transfer evidence.

Evidence: SOURCE for directly described mechanisms; REPORTED for author numbers; PROPOSED for transfer decision. No independent model reproduction. Unspecified parameters, loss, metrics, exact hardware and rights remain OPEN rather than assumed.
