# Draft relationship taxonomy — PROPOSED
Represent directed multi-label edges separately from interval geometry. Temporal: before, overlap, contains, simultaneous; identity: same occurrence vs recurring event vs same entity; discourse: answer, elaboration, explanation, evidence, contrast, concession, correction, qualification; narrative: setup, payoff, callback, reversal, escalation, resolution; pragmatic: reference, implied stance, irony, teasing; causal: asserted cause, enabling condition, inferred cause, verified cause. Separate necessity from type: essential, helpful, redundant alternative, irrelevant, contradictory, unknown. Support open descriptions and alternative interpretations. Do not infer cause from order or semantic edge from overlap. Store evidence occurrence IDs and direction, source-relative integer milliseconds, provenance, uncertainty, contradictory evidence, interpretation vs viewer necessity. Group support as OR-of-AND alternatives to handle redundancy and joint necessity. Do not equate missing observations with negative evidence.

Illustrative hypothetical: promise P, friend's launch A, realization R, sarcastic payoff J. P contradicts actual play; R establishes recognition; J may refer to P and R. Humor is a hypothesis; missing evidence of game identity means unresolved. A may be contextual but not always viewer-essential. No automatic concatenation. Tests: retrieve P and R, verify referents, remove group, restore, compare structured interpretation, equal-length unrelated control and independent raters.
