# Mathematical reconstruction and alternatives (PROPOSED except pinned source facts in S03)
Inputs: core C, source length L, candidate endpoint sets S,E, semantic units, dependency groups G, budget B, per-interval verifier. Half-open integer ms. Feasible iff 0<=s<e<=L, C subset [s,e), min<=e-s<=B, each required group has at least one fully included alternative, conditional obligations are satisfied, contextual faithfulness/closure/technical status pass. Unknown != pass. Interpretation-only evidence not automatically included. Optimize V(s,e)-lambda*(e-s)-mu*T(s,e) on feasible pairs only. Prefer Pareto frontier and independently calibrated preference; no entertainment tradeoff for distortion. Required single contiguous hull is [min starts,max ends]; if hull > B, no feasible solution under those obligations. With alternative groups and conditional obligations, hull is not sufficient. 
|Method|Algorithm / objective|Cost and training|Failure|
|---|---|---|---|
|Fixed padding|[core.start-a, core.end+b], clip to source, reject over B|O(1); no training|miss distant qualification, waste background|
|Word/segment snapping|argmin endpoint distance - alignment score over safe endpoints, then recheck|O(W), no training|can cut required semantics|
|Semantic expansion|select enclosing semantic units, union hull|O(U), no training beyond segmenter|coarse topics, interleaving|
|Learned regression|frozen features -> class/role + positive offsets; focal + IoU/regression|O(T) conv or attention-dependent; supervised|domain transfer, low uncertainty calibration|
|Pair enumeration|score every (s,e) in SxE after constraints|O(SE*G), no training; bounded endpoints|combinatorial, correlated alternatives|
|Closure hull|retrieve dependency groups; expand required hull|O(G) after retrieval, no training|wrong/missing dependencies, conditional obligations|
|Iterative shrink/expand|beam B_w over edits, reverify each; rank feasible|O(K B_w M * verifier cost); optional training|search bias, missed context, cost|
|RL|state candidate + evidence; actions expand/contract/stop/abstain, reward human preference subject to hard safety gates|rollout cost high; human feedback needed|reward hacking, invalid timestamps|
|Constrained optimization|enumerate or solve constrained interval pairs; minimize redundancy subject to closure|O(SE*G) brute; optimize indexing|incomplete feasible set|
|Hybrid|proposal heads -> retrieval -> verify -> snap -> reverify|cheap pruning plus expensive selective checks|cascade recall and verifier correlation|
Pseudocode: retrieve typed dependencies bidirectionally with budget; generate candidate endpoints (words, visual transitions, semantic units, predicted proposals); filter core, duration, source clock; evaluate each pair independently against dependencies; reject unknown or contradiction; score only passes; technical snap with constraints; reverify new interval, otherwise retain old safe cut or abstain. Cache evidence hashes and verifier versions. Stop when budgets exhausted; never convert unknown to pass. Minimum sufficient not necessarily shortest preferred. For NMS, use event identity, not IoU alone; distinct interleaved events may overlap. Proposed supervision: pairwise ranking -log sigmoid(score_good-score_bad), with distinct hard-negative labels for missing qualification. Train role head separately from interest score. No training run.
