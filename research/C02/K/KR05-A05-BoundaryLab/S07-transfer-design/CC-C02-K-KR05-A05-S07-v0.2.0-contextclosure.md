# ContextClosure alternatives (PROPOSED)
A: training-free bounded interval search over typed spans from SegmentLab and MemoryLab, verify using independent blinded LLM/human judgments; low startup cost, dependency recall and verification cost dominate. B: frozen text/speech/visual embeddings plus lightweight temporal role/offset head, train on creator-disjoint corrections, output top-K paired spans; fast proposals, requires data and calibration. C: hybrid cheap proposals and selective context retrieval, then separate closure and semantic verifier, technical snap and recheck; higher engineering complexity, cascaded error. No chosen production architecture. Compare A and C as first controlled pilots, B only after DataForge/EvalLab data gate.
