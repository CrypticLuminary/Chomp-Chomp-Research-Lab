# Boundary taxonomy
Physical: frame/shot/silence/speaker/word. Linguistic: clause/sentence/question/answer/discourse act. Semantic: thought/argument/joke/action/event. Narrative: setup/development/payoff/resolution/qualification/correction. Editorial: worthwhile hook, sufficient context, pacing, ending. Technical: alignment, padding, captions, continuity, encoding. They need not coincide. Technical endpoints should not bisect required words or actions; scene cuts may occur inside a thought. Independent audio, speech and visual timelines can overlap. Action spotting returns a point; TAL localizes labeled events; segmentation partitions timelines; GEBD spots transitions; grounding matches a query; dense captions describe events; chaptering organizes topics; summarization compresses; editing selects a standalone worthwhile excerpt with fidelity. None entails contextual closure. A sentence can be complete and still be a misleading quote. Maintain integer milliseconds [s,e), paired alternatives, core, typed required spans, interpretation-only context, abstention. S01 working definition; independent review pending.
