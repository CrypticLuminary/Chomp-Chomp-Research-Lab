# Research decision cards (all pending)
## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q001 — What makes a clip good?
- Why: Separation prevents entertainment compensating for distortion.
- Evidence: Keystone REV02; QAFactEval; KR05-F016
- Approaches: Weighted composite vs hard gates then ranking
- Support and conflict: Composite is convenient but masks fatal failures; independent gates add review cost and ambiguity.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Blind source comparison of 12 variants incl omitted qualifications; compare errors under both policies.
- Resources: 2 reviewers, 12 clip variants
- **ADOPT:** Faithfulness, standalone meaning, required context, technical/rights gates; rank interest, opening/pacing/ending/value; diversity across slate.
- Uncertainty: Rubric calibration and disagreement
- Owner: A08/A09

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q002 — Reliable human gold
- Why: Reliable labels required before tuning.
- Evidence: Krippendorff; Bradley–Terry; KR03-F007
- Approaches: Single rating vs double annotation + paired choices
- Support and conflict: Ratings support diagnostics but scale drift; paired choices relative, expensive, and can compare two bad clips.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Two raters independently score pilot; third adjudicates hard disagreements; report kappa/alpha and preference counts.
- Resources: 2 reviewers + adjudicator
- **ADOPT:** Two-pass independent review; retain uncertainty and raw ratings; no majority of AI substitutes.
- Uncertainty: Actual team agreement unknown
- Owner: A08

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q003 — Open discovery ground truth
- Why: Missing events make recall misleading.
- Evidence: Buckley/Voorhees; benchmark.py
- Approaches: Model-only pools vs exhaustive source + pooled audit
- Support and conflict: Pools efficient but miss common blind spots; exhaustive watching still subjective.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Two reviewers watch each pilot VOD before proposals; then blind pool and random negatives; record newly found events.
- Resources: 4 authorized VODs
- **ADOPT:** Independent coverage first, pool only to refine next frozen version, report known-positive recall if incomplete.
- Uncertainty: True recall unknowable on incomplete labels
- Owner: A08/A06

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q004 — Human interestingness
- Why: Replay and audio peaks not audience-independent quality.
- Evidence: Rhapsody; QVHighlights; PullQuote
- Approaches: Replay labels vs editorial choices vs engagement
- Support and conflict: Replay cheap/noisy, preferences nuanced but costly; engagement confounded by exposure.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Same candidate pool, compare heuristic, text-only, multimodal ranking using blind pairs and negative cases.
- Resources: Pilot 40–60 variants
- **ADAPT:** Use replay for weak training and diagnostic, source-independent human preference for evaluation.
- Uncertainty: Audience heterogeneity, no publication data
- Owner: A04/A08

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q005 — Contextual memory
- Why: Correct answer can be guessed without evidence.
- Evidence: LongVidSearch; KR02-F013/F014
- Approaches: Local window vs flat global vs hierarchy; supplied vs generated query
- Support and conflict: Global can retrieve distant evidence but irrelevant cost; oracle queries overstate autonomy.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: E02 and E03 with hidden needs and fixed retrieval backend; include no-support and unrelated same-entity.
- Resources: Indexed transcript/evidence, fixed budgets
- **REPRODUCE:** Evaluate missing-need detection, closure, false evidence, stop and cost independently; use flat baseline.
- Uncertainty: Real-video retrieval effectiveness unknown
- Owner: A02/A09

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q006 — Narrative relationships
- Why: Temporal correlation is not dependency.
- Evidence: NEST; KR03-F013
- Approaches: Implicit reasoning vs typed graph with verification
- Support and conflict: Typed graph inspectable but extraction errors and cost; removal can introduce artifacts.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: E05 matched anchors, near distractors, remote callbacks, oracle and detected nodes; blind grouped removal/restoration.
- Resources: Annotated units, edge and alternatives
- **REPRODUCE:** Test typed edges and semantic closure separately before graph commitment.
- Uncertainty: NEST code unavailable; human necessity disagreement
- Owner: A03/A08

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q007 — Boundary quality
- Why: High IoU can lose one decisive word.
- Evidence: KR05-F013/F014/F015; Moment-DETR
- Approaches: Fixed padding or IoU vs conditional paired closure
- Support and conflict: Structured labels expensive but preserve alternatives; tIoU cheap but insufficient.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: E06 paired candidate variants with omitted qualification, alternate setup, and snap mutation.
- Resources: 20–30 interval pairs
- **ADAPT:** Use required evidence, acceptable linked pairs, campaign constraints and independent semantic review.
- Uncertainty: Adequacy of alternative annotations
- Owner: A05/A09

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q008 — Abstention
- Why: Empty output can hide failure.
- Evidence: Keystone metrics v2; Rhapsody empty skip
- Approaches: Score only successful jobs vs assigned roster with reasons
- Support and conflict: Conditional metric useful diagnosis but optimistic if used alone.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Run synthetic and real negatives plus induced failures/partial coverage; compare strict and conditional rates.
- Resources: Complete negative/positive labels; failure fixtures
- **ADOPT:** Keep every assigned source, status and reason; false negative includes failed positive; zero precision null.
- Uncertainty: True negative VOD prevalence unknown
- Owner: A08/A09

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q009 — AI judge
- Why: Reliability != validity; self preference.
- Evidence: MT-Bench; FastChat paired order; QAFactEval
- Approaches: Sole judge vs human-only vs calibrated triage
- Support and conflict: LLM judges scalable but bias and source limitations; human workload high.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Blind two-family swapped judge evaluation vs dev human ratings; audit accepted high-risk clips.
- Resources: Pilot pairs; API approval if needed
- **ADAPT:** Deterministic checks first, AI triage after calibration, humans retain final authority.
- Uncertainty: Cost and false acceptance unknown
- Owner: A08

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q010 — Fair budget comparison
- Why: Higher quality from more compute may not be engineering gain.
- Evidence: Keystone experiments.py; LongVidSearch
- Approaches: Equal declared limits vs actual costs/Pareto
- Support and conflict: Equal spend not same access; actual latency/cost missing for subscriptions.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: E01–E08 at same relevant caps, cold and warm separately, budget sweep on dev.
- Resources: Receipts and cost counters
- **ADOPT:** Match coverage, roster, caps, renderer and available modalities except intentional intervention; publish actual cost.
- Uncertainty: Pilot caps and hardware need calibration
- Owner: A09/A08

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q011 — Initial sample size
- Why: Small pilot not statistically conclusive.
- Evidence: Creator disjoint validator; clustered inference
- Approaches: 4 VOD pilot vs immediate 24-source benchmark
- Support and conflict: Pilot inexpensive but cannot generalize; larger sample better yet annotation burden.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: 4 VOD pilot, 12 VOD expansion, estimate creator variance and learning curves.
- Resources: 28–40h pilot; ~80–120h expansion
- **ADOPT:** Pilot for instrumentation, expanded holdout for decisions, no significance claim until enough creators.
- Uncertainty: Access, rate of positives and variance unknown
- Owner: A08/A07

## CC-C02-K-KR08-Q012 — Integration gates
- Why: Prevent research demos being deployed as proven improvements.
- Evidence: PR13 REV02; all pending labs
- Approaches: Aggregate score vs per-module guardrail and E08
- Support and conflict: Single score hides tradeoffs; stage gates more work but isolates causality.
- Cheapest decisive experiment: Run E01–E08 then leave-one-component-out; preregister margins after pilot and before locked test.
- Resources: Work implementation, independent gold
- **ADOPT:** Require correctness, budget and coverage, paired practical gain and no serious distortion; Nexus approves.
- Uncertainty: Margins and sample size not yet empirically derived
- Owner: A00/A09
