# Initial literature screen — v0.1.0
Initial verified source screen, not 35–50 work target. No neural reproduction. 
1. Li et al., MAGIC-Video (arXiv v1 May 8 2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08271v1 — four node types; caption levels 30s/3min/10min/1h; six edge types; cross-modal PPR, narrative chains, controller and final answer model. Reported improvements 10.1/7.4/5.9 points over best agentic baselines on EgoLifeQA/Ego-R1/MM-Lifelong (not clipping). Appendix I actual processed 76.4 h over 14 broadcasts for Month, 51.9 h A1_JAKE; advertised benchmark extent 51 days not same as processed footage. Cost and negative NMC over-injection in Appendix L.3. ADAPT.
2. Wang et al. VideoAgent, ECCV 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10517 — LLM selects further observations through visual tools, authors report 54.1% EgoSchema and 71.3% NExT-QA with average 8.4/8.2 frames; QA-conditioned. ADAPT bounded inspection.
3. Fan et al. distinct VideoAgent, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11481 and https://videoagent.github.io/ — 2-second temporal descriptions and tracked object memory, agent tools. Do not conflate with preceding paper. ADAPT object identity hypotheses; verify tracker on stream layout.
4. MovieChat, CVPR 2024 https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Song_MovieChat_From_Dense_Token_to_Sparse_Memory_for_Long_Video_CVPR_2024_paper.html — sparse memory of visual transformer tokens, compressed rather than timestamped evidence index. DEFER until provenance and hardware audit.
5. MovieChat+ https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17176 — query-aware memory; reported MovieChat-1K with 1k videos, 2k grounding labels, 14k annotations. REPRODUCE only after rights check, not an open discovery benchmark.
6. Ego4D episodic memory https://ego4d-data.org/docs/benchmarks/episodic-memory/ — NLQ, visual and moment localization; return temporal support rather than only answer. ADAPT annotation, first-person domain gap.
7. RAPTOR, ICLR 2024 https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18059 — recursive embedding/clustering/summarization, multi-level retrieval. ADAPT with raw parallel rescue, index cost to measure.
8. HippoRAG, NeurIPS 2024 https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14831 — graph plus personalized PageRank for knowledge integration. REPRODUCE against flat index under matched budget.
9. Microsoft GraphRAG https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130 — community aggregation/global summaries for query-focused document tasks. DEFER expensive graph construction; compare local search.
10. Ego4D annotation guidelines https://ego4d-data.org/docs/data/annotation-guidelines/ — NLQ annotations include evidence windows, about 240h listed; not full 3600h corpus. ADAPT.
