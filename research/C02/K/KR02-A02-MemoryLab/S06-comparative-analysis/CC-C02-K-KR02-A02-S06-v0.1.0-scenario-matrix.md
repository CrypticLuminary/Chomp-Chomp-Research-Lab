# Scenario analysis — conceptual, PROPOSED
A long callback: global lexical/dense rescue + event timeline; nearby fails when outside window. B ambiguous pronoun: speaker scoped entity hypotheses and query expansion; entity-only may be ambiguous. C qualification: future search and stance requirement; recent-only bias risks distortion. D correction: store both attributed statements, explicit supersession only verified. E emotional payoff: retrieve setup separately, final boundary feasibility independent. F recurring object: visual embedding/instance track plus raw frame verification, name alone not identity. G parallel storylines: independent lane/time indexes, avoid forcing one L3. H false similarity: rerank evidence for exact requirement, diversify, negative controls. I silent event: modality-specific retrieval and visual coverage, not transcript absence. J overflow: cap per-candidate requirements and evidence, preserve unresolved conflict. K outside source: no fabricated context, abstain; evidence absence vs unknown. L complete: no additional retrieval unless verification warrants.
Baselines full-context, existing timestamp Director, fixed windows, BM25, lexical cosine, dense, RRF, hierarchy, entity, graph, multi-hop, ablation support scoring. E001 synthetic fixed K experiment in S08: flat methods and graph equal 10/12 essential hits; hierarchy 7/12, raw rescue 9/12, nearby 0/12 by deliberate long-range design. Not independent, no model/compute match, no superiority claims. Real benchmark requires matched source/evidence/token/frame/calls and total construction cost; include local dependencies where nearby may be best. No current evidence of graph gain; no dense embeddings executed. Do not make a graph mandatory.
