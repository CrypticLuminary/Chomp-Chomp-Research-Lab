# Proposed adaptive perception policy (shadow only)
Stage 0 authorize source, selected track, source hash, PTS clock, availability. Stage 1 continuous ASR/Director, YAMNet, optional chat and independent visual floor. Sequential low-resolution proxy at 2fps; uniform 450 semantic frames/h plus 450 nonaudio trigger/rescue at budget 900/h; jitter seeds frozen, max-gap 8s in full uniform floor. Do not call Keystone retrieve_frames once for whole hour: split <=600s/64 per request, verify global budget across batches; sequential decoder adapter proposed to avoid repeated seek overhead. Stage 2 propose typed event groups, rank each lane, no consensus gate. Stage 3 choose action among denser frames, ROI crop, temporal video encode, extra audio, earlier/later utterance, speaker context or no action. Score expected change in decision quality / measured incremental cost; initial deterministic proxy priority = unresolved required evidence + confidence disagreement + coverage gap + boundary uncertainty (dev-tuned, not calibrated probabilities). Reuse cache. Separate budgets: coverage, candidate count, total unique footage, verification, and exploration. Max 24 escalations/hour in initial shadow, max 2 per candidate, max 60s additional interval per action unless memory requests explicit longer context; global cap and protected final verification reserve. Stop when no required evidence gaps, two actions unchanged, no feasible action, or cap exhausted; unresolved/contradiction -> abstain or reviewer, not forced select. Record reasons and actual costs. 
Allocation contract requested by A09: input lane lists, stable candidate/evidence IDs, source ID, explicit lane coverage, rank/priority, K and footage/inference caps; output retained IDs and omitted reason, input/eligible/retained/omitted by lane, fully excluded and empty/unavailable lanes, used budget, dedup decisions, cache and unscanned ranges. Experimental minimum one per nonempty eligible lane if K permits, otherwise deterministic rank and report exclusion; fill with RRF after linked grouping. Do not group unrelated overlaps; equal ranks get stable ID tie break, minimum evidence validity threshold tuned dev. Exploration is not a candidate and is separately budgeted. Compare lane order/equal quota/RRF/adaptive quota in E005, no production default. 
Observation contract reuse Keystone chomp-research-evidence/1 via evidence_item: source_id, start_ms,end_ms half-open, modality (utterance/acoustic_event/chat_reaction/visual_frame/motion/subject_track/visual_region/scene_transition/ocr), model_fingerprint (weights hash/code commit/preprocessing/config), provenance (request_key, track or frame IDs and actual PTS), confidence nullable, payload labels/neutral description. Do not modify existing ID hash by later adding fields. Proposed sidecar keyed by stable evidence ID and source: observation_id, claim_id, neutral_description, evidence_refs, uncertainty {kind,reason}, coverage_id, followup_actions {interval,modality,resolution,cost_estimate}. Coverage record {source_id,lane,requested_span,observed_pts_or_spans,state: observed/not_sampled/unavailable/failed,reason,method}; claim relation supports/contradicts/unknown separate from coverage. Observed-no-detection is distinct from unavailable and does not imply absence of event. Temporal event references ordered frames, not new modality relabeling. Sidecar proposal must be approved by A09/KR01/KR02/KR03; no parallel production schema. Evidence IDs retain source-clock identity, nested events, independent claims. Exploration no gold or fake observation; verify before candidate collection.
