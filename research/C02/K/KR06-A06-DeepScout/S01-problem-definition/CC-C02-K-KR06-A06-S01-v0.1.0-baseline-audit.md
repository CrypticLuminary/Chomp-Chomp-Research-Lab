Failure hypotheses: quiet motivation and intellectual insight may be found by Director semantic scan, but density baseline can miss them and discovery budget can leave dialogue uncovered; neutral sarcasm depends on lexical/discourse context; emotional story depends on setup/payoff; silent jokes, physical actions and gameplay changes lack actual independent visual producer; delayed reaction may fall outside association windows; chat absence must not penalize; multimodal event may be split without typed evidence links. Not observed failures in independently labeled footage. Highest priority is semantic producers and coverage instrumentation, not another score.
