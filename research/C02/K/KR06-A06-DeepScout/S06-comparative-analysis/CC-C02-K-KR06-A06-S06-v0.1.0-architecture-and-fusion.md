# Architecture comparison (hypotheses, not results)
A: audio-first with independent cheap visual floor. Moderate expected quiet/silent recall, moderate false positive pressure, low/moderate compute, resilient to missing chat, low engineering complexity. Test first. B: parallel continuous semantic speech/acoustic/temporal visual models; potentially higher visual recall but costly, redundant, medium complexity; evaluate only at measured matched walltime. C: hierarchical global floor + adaptive local resolution/context; high potential but vulnerable to uncertainty gate and blind spots; higher engineering complexity. Test after A. D: full coverage end-to-end VLM chunked video; expensive, cross-chunk context limited, hallucination and modality limitations; compare feasible subset with exact token/frame budget, not oracle. All rankings qualitative, no measured superiority.
Fusion: early concatenation needs aligned features and training with modality dropout; late rank fusion works with heterogeneous scales but agreement bonus and singleton rank ties can dominate. Mandatory consensus rejects true single-lane events. Cross-modal attention/MoE needs training, masking and calibration; evidence graph preserves competing claims, but graph construction cost/edge errors must be measured. Choose candidate union with explicit event association followed by RRF and independent verification. RRF=sum 1/(60+rank_l) for genuinely linked evidence; no missing-lane penalty. Compare max rank and bounded quotas. One modality suffices for proposal, not approval. No scores converted to probability. Different overlapping stories retained; semantic same-event dedup only on provenance and validated relation. MMR only for semantic duplicates; do not penalize unfamiliar content for lacking known category.
