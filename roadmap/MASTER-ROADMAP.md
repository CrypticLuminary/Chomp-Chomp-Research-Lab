# Master roadmap

- 00 Foundation: Keystone, reproducible integration and regression checks (parallel).
- 01 Knowledge gathering: KR01–KR08 (current).
- 02 Benchmark: annotation, dataset and baseline readiness.
- 03 Intelligence experiments: semantic segmentation, memory, discovery, relationships.
- 04 Editorial reasoning: context closure, boundaries, independent verification.
- 05 Visual production: speaker identity, webcam extraction, tracking, shot composition.
- 06 Finishing: ASR, captions, mixing, hooks, performance.
- 07 Model training: only after feasibility and data gates; interest and preference learning.
- 08 Product validation: real authorized footage, review and controlled release.

Research alone does not authorize production changes. Completed VODs first, batch later, live last; zero to three distinct clips; manual publication.
