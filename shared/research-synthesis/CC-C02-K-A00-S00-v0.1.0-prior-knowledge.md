# Prior research synthesis — preliminary

Core objective: discover worthwhile, self-contained content across long VODs, including quiet speeches, opinions, qualifications, humor, social interactions and silent visual events. Audio peaks are evidence, not a definition of interest.

Prior conversation investigated Rhapsody (speech highlight training; replay-derived labels, not editorial boundaries), ClipSense (LLM experts and deterministic temporal fusion; contextual flags can be inherited rather than reverified), and cross-sector approaches: Chapter-Llama (chaptering), ChunkyEdit (thematic interview editing), GraphTP/NEST (narrative relations), MAGIC-Video/VideoAgent/Ego4D (long-video memory), pull quote selection (attention-worthy language), CALF/TimeRefine (temporal boundaries), STEPs (key-step discovery), QMSum (locate-then-summarize). See registry/source-catalog.yaml for starting links. These are leads from prior work, not newly reproduced findings.

Proposed architecture: independent semantic/audio/visual timelines, hierarchical segmentation, evidence memory, open-ended discovery, dependency resolution, semantic boundary search, independent verification, deterministic rendering. Hypotheses remain untested. Avoid mandatory modality agreement, context-free scoring, automatic fallback from real inference to mock, and forced clip selection.

Evaluation: creator-disjoint annotations; distinguish discovery recall, context completeness, faithfulness, human preference, technical quality and cost. No claims of virality without publication analytics. Verify licensing before data/model use.
